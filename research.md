# Research

Our overarching goal is to explore and understand the microorganisms in extreme environments and human systems, developing: advanced technologies for microbial detection, cultivation, and multi-omics analysis, and applying these insights to address challenges in health, environment, and biotechnology.

Here are some themes and techniques that we currently work on:

**Area 1**
trace genetic material enrichment and sequencing technologies;

**Area 2**
multi-omics software, algorithms, and analysis workflows based on artificial intelligence technologies;

**Area 3**
high-precision multi-omics data analysis and targeted enrichment cultivation techniques, in order to discover unique microbial groups during Earth's evolutionary processes and their potential applications in specialized bioproduct production, environmental restoration, and modification.
