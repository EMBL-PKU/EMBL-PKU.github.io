The PKU-EMBL Lab develops a high-throughput pipeline that transforms environmental microbial dark matter into computable knowledge, integrating extreme-environment multi-omics, artificial intelligence, and experimental validation.

Research Focus

From genes to reactions to networks
从“基因—反应—网络”的递进式技术链条

Across high-salinity, high-alkalinity, and low-temperature habitats, we connect genome reconstruction and functional annotation with metabolic reasoning and predictive community models.
