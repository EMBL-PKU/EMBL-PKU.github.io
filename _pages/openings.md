# Open Positions

The **PKU-EMBL Lab** welcomes motivated students and early-career researchers interested in **microbial dark matter, AI for life science, environmental microbiome biotechnology, and genome-resolved bioinformatics**.

We consider applications on a rolling basis for **visiting students, research assistants, master's students, Ph.D. students, and postdoctoral researchers**, subject to project fit, supervision capacity, and available support.

## Research Areas

Current and upcoming projects include:

1. **Microbial dark matter discovery** in saline-alkaline and other extreme environments
2. **Metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, and multi-omics integration** for uncultivated microorganisms
3. **AI models, scientific agents, and foundation models** for biological sequence and pathway interpretation
4. **Open-source bioinformatics software**, including genome binning, quality control, annotation, and workflow systems
5. **Microbial cultivation, functional validation, and biotechnology translation**, including biosynthetic gene clusters, antimicrobial peptides, and microbial community regulation

## Positions

**Graduate students**
Applicants interested in master's or Ph.D. training should have a strong academic record and clear motivation for interdisciplinary research in microbiome science, bioinformatics, environmental engineering, computer science, or related fields.

**Postdoctoral researchers**
Postdoctoral applicants are expected to have a strong research track record, the ability to lead independent projects, and interest in developing high-impact work at the interface of microbiology, computation, and biotechnology.

**Visiting students and research assistants**
We welcome applicants seeking rigorous research training, especially those with experience in programming, biological data analysis, molecular biology, environmental microbiology, machine learning, or scientific software development.

## Candidate Profile

Competitive applicants typically have several of the following strengths:

1. Background in **bioinformatics, computational biology, microbiology, environmental engineering, computer science, mathematics, statistics, chemistry, or related disciplines**
2. Strong quantitative thinking and reproducible research habits
3. Programming ability in **Python, R, Linux/Shell, workflow systems, or machine learning frameworks**
4. Interest in large-scale biological datasets, open-source tools, and cross-disciplinary collaboration
5. For wet-lab-oriented applicants, experience with microbial cultivation, molecular biology, omics sample preparation, or functional validation

Prior publications, research projects, software engineering experience, open-source contributions, or competition/project experience are valued but not required for all positions.

## Research Environment

The lab emphasizes **dry-wet integration**, rigorous data analysis, experimental validation, and tool building. Ongoing work connects field sampling, multi-omics sequencing, high-performance computing, AI-assisted discovery, genome-resolved microbiology, and environmental biotechnology.

The group has access to substantial computational resources, including **H200, A100, and L40S GPU systems**, and maintains active projects in software development, microbial resource mining, protein/peptide discovery, and microbiome engineering.

For selected projects, **research internships may be organized online**. Strong interns may be considered for longer-term collaboration, future graduate applications, or recommendation within the lab's training pipeline.

## 中文说明

PKU-EMBL Lab 长期欢迎有志于 **微生物“暗物质”解析、AI for Life Science、环境微生物组学、生物信息学与环境生物技术** 的同学和青年研究人员联系申请。

课题组根据项目进展与培养容量，持续接收 **访问学生、科研助理、硕士生、博士生及博士后** 等申请。

### 研究方向

1. 盐碱及其他极端环境中的微生物“暗物质”发现
2. 宏基因组、宏转录组、多组学整合与未培养微生物解析
3. 面向生命科学的 AI 模型、科学智能体与生物基础模型
4. 生物信息学开源软件、基因组分箱、质控、注释与流程系统
5. 微生物培养、功能验证、生物合成基因簇、抗菌肽与群落调控应用

### 招收对象

**研究生申请者**
欢迎具有微生物组学、生物信息学、环境工程、计算机、数学、统计、化学、生物学等背景，并希望开展长期交叉研究训练的同学联系。

**博士后申请者**
期待具有独立科研能力、良好论文或项目基础，并希望在微生物组学、AI for Science、生物信息软件、环境生物技术等方向开展高水平研究的青年学者。

**访问学生与科研助理**
欢迎希望接受系统科研训练，并具备编程、数据分析、实验操作、机器学习或软件开发经验的同学申请。合适项目可采用线上科研实习形式。

### 我们期待的能力

1. 扎实的数理、计算、生命科学或环境科学基础
2. 良好的英文阅读、科研写作和自主学习能力
3. 熟悉 Python、R、Linux/Shell、机器学习框架或生物信息分析者优先
4. 有湿实验、微生物培养、多组学样品处理或功能验证经验者优先
5. 重视长期积累、开放协作和高标准科研训练

### 平台与培养

课题组强调 **干湿结合、计算实验协同、软件工具建设与实验验证闭环**。研究内容覆盖极端环境微生物资源挖掘、多组学解析、AI for Science、生物信息软件开发、蛋白/肽发现与微生物群落调控等方向。

团队配备较强计算资源，包括 **H200、A100、L40S GPU 系统**，并可提供跨学科合作、开源项目训练、科研论文写作与成果转化相关机会。
