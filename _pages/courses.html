Courses

The PKU-EMBL Lab contributes to graduate education through courses that connect environmental science with bioinformatics, data analysis, and reproducible research. Course materials are openly available through the PKU-EMBL GitHub organization.

Graduate Course

Environmental Bioinformatics Methods

环境生物信息学方法

A research-oriented course covering biological questions, public sequence resources, alignment, metagenomic assembly and binning, genome quality assessment, functional annotation, metatranscriptomics, and amplicon-based ecological analysis.

Focus: question-driven analysis, microbial genomics, multi-omics, and reproducible computational workflows.

Project-Based Course

Methodologies of Data Visualization and Analysis

数据可视化与分析方法

A hands-on course in data exploration, analysis, visualization, and scientific communication. Student teams develop research projects using meaningful datasets and document their work through reproducible reports and public repositories.

Focus: data storytelling, Python and R workflows, team projects, and research communication.
